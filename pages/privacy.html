Plat Planner privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026. Plat Planner is a fan-made Overwolf app for Warframe, not affiliated with or endorsed by Digital Extremes.

In short: your inventory stays on your PC, encrypted. Plat Planner has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. It only contacts the services listed below, and it never sends them anything about what you own.

What Plat Planner reads

While Warframe runs, Overwolf's game events give the app your inventory (item counts and currency balances), your in-game name, and the item your cursor is over. The app never asks for chat.

What it keeps, and where

  • Item counts, balances, order tracking and settings are saved on your PC only, inside Overwolf's storage for this app. Your inventory and orders are encrypted with Windows' per-user encryption, so other Windows users on the PC can't read them. If that encryption isn't available, they aren't saved at all.
  • Your in-game name and the game's raw inventory data are never saved.
  • Market prices are cached on your PC. They're public data, not yours.

Settings > Your data > Delete my saved data erases the saved inventory and orders. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

Like any website, these services see your IP address when the app connects to them.

Ads

Plat Planner shows ads in its desktop window (never in the in-game overlay), served by Overwolf's ad platform under Overwolf's privacy policy. Overwolf's ad library may use your Overwolf profile and app information for ad targeting, which is why the app asks for Overwolf's Profile, Extensions and Streaming permissions. Plat Planner itself doesn't pass your inventory or anything else to the ad platform.

Children

Plat Planner doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date. For questions, open an issue at github.com/KDenKC/plat-planner-data.
